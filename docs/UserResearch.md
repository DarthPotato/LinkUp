### Observation 1:

- Observation Statement: Most users want help via information and preparation about their upcoming interviews or networking events.

- Supporting Data: 56.5% of user want a company briefing, 60.9% of users want questions to ask, and 65.2% of users want talking points based on their background.

- Team Action (if any): Implement said feature ideas, as the majority of users want all of them.


### Observation 2:
- Observation Statement: We have a mostly even split on whether people send follow-up emails after meetings or not

- Supporting Data: 18.4% responded Never AND Rarely, 15.8% responded I don't know what to say in one AND Always

- Team Action (if any): Set up automated follow-up emails for users after networking events they set up


### Observation #3:

- Observation Statement: Around half of the users were either in the middle or not confident when it comes to having a professional conversation.

- Supporting Data: 10.3% chose 1. not confident at all, 28.2% chose 2. Still not very confident, and 35.9% chose 3. Which is in between confident and not confident.

- Team Action (if any): Work on implementing aspects that can help users build confidence when it comes to conversations or meetings with professionals.
