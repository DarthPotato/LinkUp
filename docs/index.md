### App Name
**LinkUp**

### Short Description
Practice networking and interview prep with AI-powered coaching.

### Full Description
LinkUp helps you get ready for networking events, coffee chats, and interviews with practical, personalized preparation tools.  

Use LinkUp to:
- Build confidence before important conversations
- Generate meeting prep briefs from your goals
- Practice likely questions and improve your responses
- Track progress and refine your communication over time

LinkUp is designed for students, early-career professionals, and anyone who wants to connect with confidence.
